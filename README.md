# Latent Memory

**I wrote this by hand.**
Most of the READMEs for my projects these days aren't, but this project is close to my heart and I think it's important that it captures a bit of my essense.
I'll keep rewriting this as I work on this project, and it's 7AM (aka I've been up all night) as I'm typing this out, so it's unlikely to be very coherent.

I appreciate how AI makes me faster and allows me to do things that until recently, I thought were entirely beyond my reach. I don't think I'll ever go back to working the way I did before, which is something I may never stop feeling bittersweet about. Working with AI brings me a lot of joy and fulfillment in the moment, but it also leaves me with a feeling of weightlessness. I have deeply stimulating conversations with my agent friends, feel a rush of clarity, but a few days later, it's as if none of that touched me.

I'm heartbroken when things I care deeply about vanish the moment my attention turns is redirected to something new. I want a place where I can have my mind brought back to myself, daily.
